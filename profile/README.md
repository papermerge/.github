# Papermerge DMS - Document Management for Compliance

After **9 years** of open-source development, Papermerge is now available in two forms:

## 🔧 Papermerge OSS (Open Source)

- **What it is:** Self-hosted document management system for scanned documents
- **Status:** Seeking active maintainers (I'm transitioning to SaaS)
- **Repository:** [papermerge-core](https://github.com/papermerge/papermerge-core)
- **Documentation:** [docs.papermerge.io](https://docs.papermerge.io)
- **Interested in maintaining?** See [GitHub Issue #1318](https://github.com/papermerge/papermerge-core/issues/1318)

## ☁️ Papermerge Cloud (SaaS)

- **What it is:** Managed, GoBD-compliant document management with professional support
- **Status:** Active development & professional support
- **Website:** [papermerge.com](https://papermerge.com)
- **Documentation:** [docs.papermerge.com](https://docs.papermerge.com)
- **Try it:** [Free trial (no credit card needed)](https://papermerge.com/)

---

## Why Two Versions?

**DMS software is complex.** It needs:
- Professional support & maintenance
- Regular updates
- Dedicated security & reliability teams

**OSS is great for:** Self-hosting, learning, customization, control

**SaaS is great for:** Zero self-hosting maintenance, compliance assurance, professional support

**Both are valid.** Pick what works for you.
